Annotation guide

Hoop / rim

  • Label only the scoring hoop involved in this play.
  • Draw a tight box around the rim.
  • Exclude the backboard and most of the net.
  • After propagation, correct its position when the camera moves.

Basketball

  • Draw tightly around the visible game ball.
  • Do not include a hand, shadow, or extra surrounding space.
  • Never guess its position. Leave the basketball box absent when the ball is not visible.

Useful sequence

  • Include approach, above-rim, rim-overlap, below-rim, and downward-continuation frames.
  • Generate detailed frames around the actual rim crossing.
  • The event moment is the completed passage through the rim, not shot release or first contact.
  • Leave the basketball absent when it is not visible; mark unusable frames Skipped.

Box size and placement

  • Click once to place a box using the last size used for that object.
  • Drag on empty space to draw a custom box and remember its new size.
  • Select a box and drag its white corner handle to resize it. The resized dimensions become the new remembered size.
  • Remembered basketball and hoop sizes are separate and apply only to this recording.

Frame review status

  • Pending: the frame has not been fully checked.
  • Reviewed: the frame is usable and complete. A reviewed frame may intentionally have no basketball box when the ball is not visible.
  • Skipped: exclude a blurred, corrupted, transitional, or otherwise unusable frame from training.

Sequence outcome

  • Use Basket attempt events for both made and missed training examples.
  • Made: the ball completes its passage through the rim.
  • Missed: a shot approaches the scoring area but does not pass through.
  • Uncertain: the outcome cannot be verified confidently.
  • The game list displays a reviewed basket attempt as Made basket or Missed basket.

Detailed frames

  • Generate detailed frames only around the likely rim crossing.
  • Purple timeline markers identify 0.1-second detailed frames.
  • Use a detailed frame’s removal button if the group was generated around the wrong moment.
  • Removing a detailed group never removes the original broad frames.

Select frames for propagation

  • Choose Select frames, then click the timeline frames you want to include.
  • The first selected frame is the source. Draw or confirm its hoop and basketball boxes first.
  • Fill missing hoop or basketball copies from the source only into the selected frames.
  • A frame that already contains the selected object type is skipped; existing boxes are never replaced or duplicated.
  • With no selection, propagation continues to fill all missing frames from the current frame.

Queue and skipping

  • Use the queue to move directly between highlights; unsaved changes are saved before switching.
  • Needs work includes highlights that are not started or still in progress.
  • Mark a usable frame Reviewed even when the ball is intentionally absent.
  • Mark unusable frames Skipped. Skipped frames are excluded from training exports.
  • Use Skip selected frames for a chosen set, or Skip remaining pending frames when the rest of a highlight is unusable.
  • A highlight with every frame skipped is treated as Unusable and no longer appears under Needs work.

Test a trained model

  • Select a completed model and run the test directly on the current highlight window.
  • Dashed model boxes are predictions; solid boxes are your annotations.
  • Green timeline markers passed the comparison. Red markers contain a missed annotation or extra model detection.
  • The comparison uses box overlap, crossing outcome, and the known highlight time. It never creates another highlight.
  • Use unseen games for trustworthy evaluation; results on training footage can look better than real-world performance.
